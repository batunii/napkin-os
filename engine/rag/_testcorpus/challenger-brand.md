## Summary
A challenger brand wins not by out-spending the leader but by changing the
frame of the category so the leader's size becomes a liability.

## When to use
Use when your brand has low salience, a smaller budget, and faces an entrenched
incumbent. Especially potent for a nervous decision-maker who fears gimmicks.

## The core move
Pick a fight with the category convention, not the competitor. Name the tension
the leader can't acknowledge without undermining itself.

## Proof
Effectiveness data shows challenger framing lifts consideration faster than
share-of-voice matching when budgets are asymmetric.
