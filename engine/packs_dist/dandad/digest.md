## What great looks like
* **Reframing the familiar**: Strong work often involves taking a well-known concept or problem and reframing it in a way that unlocks new possibilities or perspectives, such as transforming a simple object into a tool for social change or using technology to reimagine traditional experiences.
* **Emotional resonance**: Successful strategies often tap into universal human emotions, creating a deep connection with the audience, whether through storytelling, visual aesthetics, or interactive experiences that evoke feelings of wonder, empathy, or inspiration.
* **Simplification through innovation**: Great work can simplify complex problems or needs by introducing innovative solutions, materials, or technologies that make the seemingly impossible accessible, such as turning a bottle cap into a water purification system or creating immersive experiences that educate and entertain.

## Craft rules for a brief
* **Start with a clear problem statement**: A well-defined problem or opportunity statement is crucial for focusing the creative effort and ensuring that the solution addresses a real need or desire.
* **Encourage multidisciplinary approaches**: Combining insights and skills from various disciplines can lead to more innovative and effective solutions, as seen in projects that blend art, technology, and social impact.
* **Prioritize emotional connection**: Briefs should aim to create an emotional resonance with the target audience, recognizing that decisions are often influenced by feelings as much as by facts, and that storytelling and aesthetic appeal can be powerful tools in this regard.

## Traps
* **Overreliance on technology**: While technology can be a powerful tool for innovation, overreliance on it can lead to solutions that are overly complex or that neglect the human element, resulting in a lack of emotional resonance or practical usability.
* **Neglecting the human factor**: Solutions that do not consider the emotional, social, or cultural contexts of their users can fail to engage or be relevant, highlighting the importance of empathy and understanding in the design process.
* **Lack of authenticity**: Strategies or solutions that are perceived as insincere or that do not align with the values or mission of the brand or organization can undermine trust and credibility, emphasizing the need for authenticity and transparency in all communications and actions.
