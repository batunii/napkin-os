Subject: Lúnasa 0.0 — launch brief

Hi all,

As discussed, we're launching Lúnasa 0.0, an alcohol-free version of Lúnasa, in Ireland and Northern Ireland/GB next spring.

The problem, in a sentence: people who love Lúnasa are drinking less of it midweek, and when they choose an alcohol-free option they reach for a beer, not a cider.

What we need: Lúnasa 0.0 to be the alcohol-free cider people ask for by name, winning back the midweek occasions we're losing.

We'd like it to reach 5% of our total volume in Ireland within the first year.

Who it's for: existing Lúnasa drinkers who are cutting back, plus anyone who has gone alcohol-free and misses cider.

We see Brightwater 0.0 as the one to beat.

Budget is in the region of €400k working media and production across both markets.

We need a 30" TVC with a BVOD cut, social cutdowns and 48-sheet posters. TV and BVOD are a must.

Mandatories: the Lúnasa sunburst and the 0.0 badge must be on every piece, and no creative may show anyone who is or looks under 25.

Thanks,
Niamh
Brand Manager, Glenmore Drinks
