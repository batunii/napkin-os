Everything recordedThe ask, every pinned fact, the findings and what research drew on. Who decided what, and why, is in Decisions.

The ask

What the client wants done. Every field says how it got here: you said this is the client's own material, quoted; we inferred this is derived from pinned facts. A field nobody has filled stays empty, with the gate that waits on it.

Pinned facts

Frozen copies of knowledge-layer rows. The document owns no facts; it pins them. A pin never follows a newer version on its own — a stale pin is flagged and the current version offered.

Findings

The points drawn from the pins. Each stays a proposal until a person agrees to it; nothing unverified enters the layers.

Selection

What research drew on, lens by lens and market by market; what it looked for and did not find; what two sources could not agree on; and what it chose to leave out.
